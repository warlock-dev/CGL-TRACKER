How the schedule works — the day you mark a topic "read", it's due back for a quick pass after 1 day. Each time you revise it on schedule, the gap widens: 1 → 3 → 7 → 15 → 30 → 60 days, following the standard spaced-repetition curve for exam retention. Five revisions and a topic is marked mastered, but it still resurfaces every 60 days to keep it from fading. If a revision is missed, the schedule itself doesn't reset — it just keeps nudging you daily until you catch up. Everything here is saved to your account automatically. For Mayank, connecting Google Calendar above keeps a matching reminder on your calendar for every topic in the revision cycle, moved and relabelled automatically as you progress.
